Care built on trust, not turnover

HealthCare Plus Clinic opened in 2011 with one goal: give every patient the kind of unhurried, coordinated care we'd want for our own families.

Our story

Started by three doctors tired of ten-minute appointments

HealthCare Plus began as a single family-medicine practice founded by Dr. Imran Qureshi, Dr. Farhan Malik, and Dr. Nadia Hussain, who shared a frustration with rushed, disconnected care.

Fifteen years later, we've grown into a multi-specialty clinic with 42 physicians — but we've kept the founding rule: every doctor reads your full history before you walk in, and every visit ends with a clear plan, not just a prescription.

Today we see over 20,000 patients a year across general medicine, pediatrics, cardiology, dermatology, orthopedics, and dental care.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, coordinated, and genuinely attentive healthcare — treating the person, not just the appointment slot.

Our Vision

To be the clinic our community turns to first — known as much for how we listen as for how we treat.
