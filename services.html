How it works

Booking takes four steps

1

Choose a department

Pick the specialist care you need, or let our front desk guide you.

2

Pick a time

Select from same-day and next-day slots shown on the booking form.

3

Get confirmed

We confirm by email or SMS within one business day, often sooner.

4

Visit & follow up

See your specialist, then get a written plan and any needed follow-ups.

Insurance & payment

Most major Pakistani insurers accepted

We work with State Life, EFU Health, Jubilee General, and Adamjee Insurance, and offer transparent self-pay pricing for patients without coverage. Our billing team will confirm your estimated out-of-pocket cost before any procedure.

Direct insurance billing

We file claims on your behalf with State Life, EFU Health, Jubilee General, and Adamjee.

Flexible payment plans

Split larger procedure costs over several months, interest-free.
